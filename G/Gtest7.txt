Problem G
Genealogy Gumbo
Time limit: 3 seconds
A reconstruction of the family tree Ja$on has found. CC BY-SA 4.0
by Shakko on Wikimedia
Jay’s son ja$on recently discovered that he is the latest member of the leg-
endary JSON Dynasty. Now, he’s trying to prove a research hypothesis: that
the entire dynasty is descended from a single common ancestor.
Sadly, because his ancestors are long dead, he can’t ask them any questions.
Instead, he has to rely on documents left from that era where names are writ-
ten patrilineally: ancestry is only traced by the name of someone’s father.
One such example would be “Jayden, son of Jacob”.
In order to prove his hypothesis, Ja$on needs to determine whether such a
family tree exists: a family tree containing all of his ancestors, with a single
root. You cannot create imaginary people or construct familial relations for
which there is no historical evidence. One father can have multiple children,
but one child cannot have multiple fathers. It is possible that different people have the same name.
Input
The input consists of:
• One line with an integer n (1 ≤ n ≤ 105), the number of parental relations specified.
• n lines, each with a name of the form “A, son of B”.
Each name has at most 20 characters and consists of one English uppercase letter (A-Z), followed by only English
lowercase letters (a-z).
Output
If it is possible for the given people to have a single common ancestor, output “possible”. Otherwise, output
“impossible”.
Sample Input 1 Sample Output 1
3
Jacob, son of Isaac
Isaac, son of Abraham
Ishmael, son of Abraham
possible
Sample Input 2 Sample Output 2
2
Basil, son of Alexios
Procopius, son of Constantine
impossible
