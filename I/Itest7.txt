Problem I
Ingredient Intervals
Time limit: 1 second
The ingredient list of a stroopwafel, where only two
percentages are shown.
The label of a supermarket product describes the composition of its ingredients as
follows:
Ingredients: Chocolate (41.1%), Sugar, Peanuts (20%), Starch, Thick-
ener, Glaze.
In accordance with regulations from the Section of Product Assembly and Review
(SPAR), the ingredients are listed in descending order by percentage, and the per-
centages add up to 100%. While some ingredients have their percentage specified
on the label, others do not.
For each unspecified ingredient, determine the minimum and maximum possible
percentage, given these conditions.
Input
The input consists of:
• One line with an integer n (1 ≤ n ≤ 100), the number of ingredients on the label.
• n lines, each starting with a string w (1 ≤ |w| ≤ 20), the name of the ingredient, optionally followed by a
single number p (0 < p ≤ 100), the specified percentage of the ingredient.
The ingredient names are unique and only consist of English lowercase letters (a-z). Percentages are given with
at most one digit after the decimal point. There is at least one ingredient whose percentage is not specified. The
input describes a valid label.
Output
Output the unspecified ingredients in the order given in the input. For each such ingredient, output its name,
followed by its minimum and maximum percentages.
Your answer should have an absolute or relative error of at most 10−6.
